Model notes

Scope & sequence

A semester of engineering, physics, biology, and chemistry. The original plan spans 16 weeks; these phases are flexible.

Teaching approach & assessment

Launch. Investigate. Explain.

Introduce a problem, build or test an idea, then use evidence to explain what happened.

Offer sketches, oral explanations, and writing. The worksheets include sentence starters and, in most cases, Spanish prompts. Reduce support as students take ownership.
