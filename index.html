About this space

Learning is something
we make together.

This is a public window into Mr. K’s classroom: a place to follow projects as they develop, explore the thinking behind them, and find the materials that make them possible.

The work is ongoing. New observations, revisions, and exhibitions will appear as our classroom projects grow.
